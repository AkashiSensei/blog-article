# Cloud and AI Infra

More precisely, this category focuses on cloud-native platform engineering and system practice for AI, rather than full-stack AI infrastructure. It covers less about feature engineering, training frameworks, and similar implementation details, and focuses more on platform capabilities such as GPU and NPU resource management, containerized development environments, and workflow or job orchestration. The goal is to provide simple and easy-to-use computing resources for AI workers.

Most articles here may be based on Crater, a Kubernetes-oriented AI development platform maintained by our project group.
